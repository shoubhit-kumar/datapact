================================================================
SYSTEM OVERVIEW
================================================================

The Municipal Inspection Management System (MIMS) tracks all building, food,
industrial, and healthcare inspections conducted by the City Compliance Division.

The system was originally built in 2018 on a legacy Oracle DB and migrated to
Azure SQL in 2024. Some data quality issues from the migration are documented
in the Jira project DEV (see issue log).

Primary users:
- Field inspectors (mobile app)
- Supervisors (web dashboard)
- City compliance officers (reports)
- Data team (pipelines and analytics)

================================================================
ZONE CLASSIFICATION
================================================================

Zone A — High Risk
  Includes: hospitals, large food processing units, chemical storage,
  buildings over 20 years old with no recent structural inspection.
  Inspection frequency: every 90 days mandatory.
  Overdue threshold: 100 days (10-day grace period).

Zone B — Medium Risk
  Includes: mid-size commercial buildings, schools, mid-scale food outlets.
  Inspection frequency: every 180 days.
  Overdue threshold: 200 days.

Zone C — Low Risk
  Includes: small shops, residential buildings under 10 years old.
  Inspection frequency: annually (365 days).
  Overdue threshold: 400 days.

IMPORTANT: Sites with NULL zone_code were registered before the zone system
was introduced (pre-2022). They default to Zone B treatment operationally
but should be formally reclassified. As of Feb 2025, ~3400 sites are
still unclassified.

================================================================
INSPECTION SCORING
================================================================

Score range: 0 to 100.
- 85 and above: PASS
- 60 to 84: PARTIAL (subject to re-inspection within 30 days)
- Below 60: FAIL (immediate action required)
- NULL: inspection was started but not completed

overall_status must be consistent with score:
  score >= 85 → PASS
  score 60-84 → PARTIAL or PASS (inspector discretion for borderline)
  score < 60  → FAIL
  score NULL  → DEFERRED (inspection to be rescheduled)

Any record where overall_status contradicts score range is a data quality error.

================================================================
INSPECTOR LICENSING
================================================================

All inspectors must hold a valid government license (LIC-YYYY-NNNNNN format).
License numbers are issued by the State Compliance Authority.

Cert levels and their permissions:
  L1 — Can inspect Zone C sites only
  L2 — Can inspect Zone B and Zone C sites
  L3 — Can inspect any zone
  SENIOR — Can inspect any zone + can sign off on re-inspections

An inspector assigned to a zone outside their certification level is a
compliance violation and must be flagged immediately.

License expiry: An inspector with an expired license must not be assigned
new inspections. The system should check this daily.

================================================================
KNOWN DATA RELATIONSHIPS (not enforced in DB)
================================================================

inspection_records.site_id → inspection_sites.site_id
  Every inspection must be for a registered site.
  Orphan records (site_id not in inspection_sites) indicate manual entry bypass.

inspection_records.inspector_id → inspectors.inspector_id
  Every inspection must be conducted by a registered inspector.
  18 orphan records found in 2024 (see INC-2024-0183).

inspectors.supervisor_id → inspectors.inspector_id
  Supervisor must also be an inspector in the system.
  Not all inspectors have a supervisor (senior-most level can be NULL).

================================================================
BUSINESS RULES ENFORCED IN APPLICATION (not in DB)
================================================================

1. An inspector cannot inspect a site they own or have a declared conflict of interest with.
2. A FAIL inspection must have at least 1 critical finding (critical_findings >= 1).
3. If followup_required = 1, followup_deadline must be within 30 days of inspection_date.
4. A site with status INACTIVE (is_active = 'false') must not have any new inspections.
5. report_submitted must be 'Yes' within 48 hours of inspection_date.
6. score and overall_status must be consistent (see scoring table above).
7. inspector cert_level must cover the site zone_code at time of inspection.

These rules are enforced by the application layer but NOT by database constraints.
DataPact should generate DQ rules to catch violations in the data layer.
