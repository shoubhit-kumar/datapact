[2024-11-14 09:30] Kavya QA
QA flag — duration_hrs in inspection_records. We found values of 0.0 and also
values above 24. An inspection cannot take 0 hours or more than a full day.

[2024-11-14 09:45] Priya Nair
Agreed. duration_hrs should be between 0.5 and 12.0. Anything outside
that is a data entry error. Please add to DQ rules.

[2024-11-14 10:09] Priya Nair
Yes — a PASS inspection with 0 findings is valid. But a FAIL must have
findings_count >= 1. And critical_findings must always be <= findings_count.
That's a hard rule.
